# Portfolio

Shashintha Edirisooriya's personal portfolio website, aimed at people hiring for Full Stack and AI/LLM roles in the UK and EU.

## Language

**Portfolio**:
The new website being designed here, which replaces the old Bootstrap site in `~/myportfolio`.
_Avoid_: Site (on its own), web app, homepage

**Portfolio spec**:
The document that describes the Portfolio completely enough to build it without making further decisions. It's the destination of this effort.
_Avoid_: Plan, design doc, PRD

**Reader**:
A recruiter or hiring manager who opens the Portfolio while screening for a Full Stack or AI/LLM role.
_Avoid_: User, visitor, client

**Master CV**:
The CV at `~/Dev/CV/deliverables/cv/Shashintha-Edirisooriya-CV.html`, and the only source for what the Portfolio claims.
_Avoid_: Resume, my data

**Claim rules**:
The limits on what may be said, carried over from the Master CV. The AI work at Albhadreen is described as experimental only. The F1-score of 0.82 is the only number. There are no MCP claims, and no skill appears that the Master CV doesn't list.

**Scan test**:
The check that a Reader can find the current role, stack, right to work and projects within 10 seconds, on a phone or a desktop. It's the Portfolio's top priority, ahead of proof of skill and then memorability.

**Proof of skill**:
Engineering quality that the Portfolio shows in itself: performance, accessibility, tests, and a working fallback when 3D can't run.

**Fact strip**:
The row of short labelled facts at the top of the Portfolio, always in the first screen: Now, Stack, Based, Right to work and Education.
_Avoid_: Info bar, stats

**Featured**:
The few projects shown in full, with a 3D gallery panel and a write-up.
_Avoid_: Highlights, top projects

**Archive**:
The short list of lesser projects shown as one line each, with no 3D.
_Avoid_: Other projects, misc

**Signature moment**:
The one 3D interaction a Reader is meant to remember. It must never get in the way of the Scan test.
_Avoid_: Wow factor, hero animation
