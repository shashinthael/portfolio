Zeylan: trip planner for Sri Lanka holidays

Personal project · Nov 2026 · live site ↗

Visitors choose who's travelling, for how long, what they love and a comfort level, then see a price estimate and the best-matching route on a 3D map of the island.

The 3D island loads only when it's on screen and falls back to a static map. Enquiries go through a Cloudflare Worker with a Turnstile spam check, a D1 database and email through Resend.

Stack: TypeScript, Astro, three.js, Cloudflare Workers, D1, Turnstile, Resend
